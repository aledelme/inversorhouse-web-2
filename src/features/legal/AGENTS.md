# legal — legal notice, terms, privacy, cookies

- Long-form texts live in `content/<locale>.tsx` (not in messages). Owner data comes from
  `brand.legalOwner`; bump `brand.legalUpdatedAt` whenever a text changes.
- Any change to these texts must be reviewed by a lawyer before publishing. If a feature changes
  what personal data is processed or shared (e.g. new contact fields), update `Privacy` and `Terms`.
